Overview

Sweet Cypress Ranch ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, sign up for the SCR Stars Club portal, or interact with our in-barn QR ordering system.

Information We Collect

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, mobile phone number, and delivery address.
  • Account Credentials: Passwords and location preferences associated with your SCR Stars Club account.
  • Transaction & Inventory Data: Order history, preferred feed SKUs, and pick-up or delivery selections.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders, manage warehouse pick tickets, and coordinate local deliveries or dock pickups.
  • To send transactional updates, order confirmations, pickup alerts, and item substitution requests via SMS and email.
  • To maintain your SCR Stars Club membership perks and rewards fund eligibility.

Important: SMS & Mobile Information Sharing Policy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this personal information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances, except as required by law or to provide the direct transactional services you requested.
