SCR Stars Club SMS Program
Last Updated: November 15, 2026
Program Name: SCR Stars Club Notifications & Order Alerts

Program Description

By joining the SCR Stars Club portal, scanning in-barn QR codes, or providing your mobile phone number, you expressly opt-in to receive automated operational messages from Sweet Cypress Ranch. These include:

  • Order confirmations and status updates
  • Pickup readiness alerts at our Fort Myers or LaBelle docks
  • Item substitution approval requests and hay availability notifications
  • Delivery schedule updates

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your active orders, re-orders, and farm supply needs.

Cost & Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details regarding your text messaging plan.

How to Opt-Out (STOP)

You can cancel or unsubscribe from the SMS service at any time. Simply text STOP to our toll-free messaging number. [TOLL-FREE NUMBER]

Upon sending STOP, we will send you a final SMS confirmation reply confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You will no longer receive automated text messages unless you re-opt in.

How to Get Help (HELP)

If you experience any issues or need assistance with our messaging program, reply HELP for customer support instructions, or contact us directly at:

  • Email: support@sweetcypressranch.com
  • Phone: LaBelle (863) 517-2079 · Fort Myers (239) 690-0229

Compatible carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon Wireless, Sprint, Boost, U.S. Cellular, and minor regional carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
